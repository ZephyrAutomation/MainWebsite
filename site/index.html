What we build

Six ways we take work off your plate.

Every engagement starts with the same question: where is your team spending hours on something a system should be doing? Then we build exactly that — nothing more.

01

AI Agents & LLM Integration

Agents that read, decide, and act inside the systems you already run — grounded in your own data and checked against real evaluations before they touch production.

02

Workflow & Process Automation

Intake, approvals, reporting, reconciliation. We map the process, delete the handoffs, and leave it running unattended with a clear audit trail.

03

Custom Software Engineering

Where off-the-shelf stops, we build. Internal tools, APIs, and services written to be read, tested, and maintained by whoever comes next.

04

Data Pipelines & Integrations

Get your systems talking. Observable pipelines that move clean data from source to warehouse to dashboard, and tell you the moment they don't.

05

AI Strategy & Consulting

A prioritised roadmap of where automation actually pays — each opportunity sized by effort, risk, and return, so you can fund the right one first.

06

Managed Support & Monitoring

Automation isn't fire-and-forget. Alerting, tuning, and a named engineer on call, so a silent failure never becomes a quarter-end surprise.

How it goes

Small, working, then bigger.

No twelve-week discovery phase before anything runs. We ship something real early, prove it against your numbers, and expand from there.

    Find the bottleneck

    A short working session on your actual process. We leave with the hours, the handoffs, and the one thing worth automating first.

  2. 02

    Build the first slice

    One narrow, end-to-end automation in weeks rather than quarters — running on your data, in your stack, with a human in the loop.

  3. 03

  4. 04

    Monitoring, retraining, and documentation handed over — so the system keeps earning long after we've stopped invoicing for it.
